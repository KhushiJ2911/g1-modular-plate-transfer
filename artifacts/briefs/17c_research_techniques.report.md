# Move Plate — best bets for reaching 90% today

**Freeze the 71% BC policy and train a small, gated correction for the phase where it can rescue the most episodes.** You need 19 net rescues per 100 episodes, roughly two-thirds of baseline failures if existing successes hold. I found no published ≥90% Move Plate result under your observation limits. BiGym’s pre-release tool-augmented runs scored **93/100, 18/100, and 12/100**, using 640×480 images and world-frame wrist and base positions. [BiGym 2.0, Table A8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07594v3#A7.SS2)

At 45 seconds per episode, reserve **75 minutes for the final 100 seeds**. That leaves at most 540 full serial episodes before implementation and training. Eight hours fits $15 at an all-in rate of $1.875/hour. Google Cloud lists the T4 accelerator at $0.35/hour before VM charges. [GPU pricing](https://cloud.google.com/products/compute/gpus-pricing)

## Ranked by expected gain per hour

1. **Selective supervised residual — best same-day bet.** On LIBERO-Goal, frozen ACT went **70.0% → 87.0% → 91.2%** with one residual head and then four bounded heads plus a learned gate that could retain the base action. That’s **350 → 435 → 456 successes out of 500**, 50 trials/task. Drawer-open then bowl-place went **66% → 98%**. Bottle-to-rack went **18% → 86%**. Training used 338 demo trajectories, 90 validation trajectories, zero online rollouts, and 500 residual epochs. Cached updates took 3.44 ms/batch of eight on a 5090. Full wall-clock time was unreported. **Plausible today:** cache ResNet features and train bounded corrections for the active arm and gripper, with BC as the default. [Selective Residual Action Chunking, Tables 2–3](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/21/6992)

2. **Sweep execution settings while collecting diagnostics.** DASH-ACT improved simulated moka-pot placement **68.0% → 91.3%**, using 50 starts × three training seeds. Both predictors trained for 5,000 epochs. The baseline committed 20 actions, and a post-hoc fixed horizon on the DASH predictor slightly beat adaptive selection across tasks. **Plausible in under an hour:** screen recent-only versus latest-only predictions near contact and a gripper debounce of 3–5 frames. I found no comparable gain measured against an already-ensembled policy. [DASH-ACT](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/26/22/7239)

3. **DAgger-style corrections if you have a training oracle.** I3L’s simulated strawberries went **53.2% → 75.6% after 25 corrections → 92.8% after 100**. Fridge-and-radio went **39.6% → 90.0% after 100**. Each condition had 250 autonomous evaluations. The oracle used simulator state, and each round retrained for 200k diffusion-policy or 40k π₀.₅ updates. Physical three-camera Res-HIL cable insertion went **70% → 92% in 10 minutes → 100% at 45 minutes**, with human corrections and 50 starts × three runs. **Plausible today with existing IK:** collect targeted corrections in batches of 25 and warm-start the small head. A hundred full episodes cost 75 minutes before training. [I3L](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17748v1#S4), [Res-HIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30023#S4)

4. **Rerank a few smooth corrections at the failure point.** TeV lifted bimanual RoboTwin2 **72.3% → 81.3%** over 700 trials per condition, using four candidates and existing demos. Q-Planning lifted RoboTwin **83.2% → 83.8% offline → 91.4% online** over 940 trials per condition. Its 1,000 online episodes/task alone would take 12.5 hours here, plus critic pretraining on 4×H200. **Plausible today in miniature:** fork training snapshots with 3–5 bounded candidates, including BC, and fit a small RGB/proprio scorer to branch outcomes. I found no budget-matched ≥90% result for generic reranking. [TeV](https://hatchetproject.github.io/tev/), [Q-Planning](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21204v1#S4)

5. **ResFiT if TD3 is already wired.** Fixed-base humanoid Coffee went **77% → 98%** with ResFiT, versus **0%** for Tuned RLPD. The released config uses 50 evaluation episodes/checkpoint. The exact Coffee denominator was unconfirmed. BoxCleanup converged at **200k steps versus 40M** for residual PPO, with 1,000 demos. Square exceeded **90% by 150k** with ResFiT and Tuned RLPD. Pixel IBRL solved Square within **500k** using 50 demos and 50 evals/checkpoint in released code. Exact IBRL endpoints were unreported. **Conditional today:** 200k steps cost roughly 1.5 hours of simulation at your full-episode throughput, before updates. Use frozen features and phase-local exploration. [ResFiT](https://residual-offpolicy-rl.github.io/), [ResFiT config](https://github.com/amazon-far/residual-offpolicy-rl/blob/main/resfit/rl_finetuning/config/rlpd.py#L219-L268), [IBRL](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.02198#S5), [IBRL config](https://github.com/hengyuan-hu/ibrl/blob/main/release/cfgs/robomimic_rl/square_ibrl.yaml)

6. **Defer full residual PPO, diffusion steering, and ConRFT.** ResiP’s 50 Hz bimanual plate insertion went **33% → 93%** over 1,024 trials using privileged state and up to 500M training steps. Policy Decorator’s state-based StackCube went **71% → near 100%** by its plot, with 50 evals × ten seeds and a 5M-step timing run. DSRL’s physical spoon placement went **15/20 → 19/20** after 65 online episodes, but needs a diffusion or flow policy. ConRFT’s physical banana task went **65% → 100%**, 20 trials per condition. Its main pipeline used 80–120 intervention rollouts and 45–90 minutes online on an A6000. Plain success-filtered BC is cheap to try, but ResFiT found minimal gains and I found no comparable **~71% → ≥90%** result. [ResiP](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.16677), [Policy Decorator](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13630), [DSRL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v305/wagenmaker25a.html), [ConRFT](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05450#S5), [Filtered BC](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19301v2#S5.SS1)

**First 30 minutes:** run 40 development episodes and label grasp, transport, seating, and release failures. Fork just before the likely bottleneck with full simulator and controller state **during training**. Pick the correction with the most paired net rescues on separate validation seeds. Deploy from RGB and proprioception, keep the 50-step success hold inside 1,700 steps, and freeze the policy before the final evaluation.
