# Move Plate public code and results

I found **no public ≥90% Move Plate solution under your 84×84 RGB and proprioception rules**, and no qualifying ≥90% result for the original H1 task. The most actionable reuse for your 71% policy is the released grasp-retry and placement feedback code. The paper’s **93/100** result came from a prerelease setup with extra sensing and robotics tools. [BiGym 2.0 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07594v3) · [Live leaderboard](https://bigym2.github.io/#leaderboard)

1. **Borrow feedback from the released controllers.** Astra s1 scored **36/100** without IK. It centers the plate rim in the left wrist image, docks using head-camera rack features and pelvis proprioception, and detects a shallow pinch from finger positions. Its recovery reopens, advances **3.5 cm**, and regrips once. It also uses persistent joint tracking error as a seating cue. Opus s3 scored **23/100** with numerical IK. It aligns the rim, descends along the tool axis, lifts and backs off, then stops and remeasures the destination rack before lowering, releasing, and retracting. Both contain timed phases. For today, use your BC on development seeds, label failures by grasp, carry, and seating, then add feedback at the phase costing the most successes. Use the shipped Dex1-1 geometry for IK and choose a visible interior slot. Any of six slots counts within **5 cm** and **20°** of the required right-facing orientation. [Astra s1 source](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SWIRL-Lab/bigym2-agent-rollouts/blob/main/policies/move_plate/astra-s1/policy.py) · [Opus s3 source and helpers](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SWIRL-Lab/bigym2-agent-rollouts/tree/main/policies/move_plate/opus-s3) · [Task criteria](https://bigym2.github.io/docs/tasks.html#move-plate)

2. **Use the agent traces for diagnosis.** The CC BY 4.0 release has **21 Move Plate programs** with per-seed CSVs and **600 full-state/action rollouts** from the six paper programs. Across all tasks it indexes **420 sessions**, with **5,400** full paper rollouts and **5,347** matched line traces. Paper Opus scored **21/15/23 out of 100** with IK, and Astra high scored **36/2/4 out of 100** without it. The November 19 leaderboard lists **Sonnet 5.5 max 54%** and **Astra max 25%**, each over three programs × 100 episodes. I found no Sonnet program or per-session breakdown. The prerelease tool-assisted Astra sessions scored **93/18/12 out of 100**, versus **31/40/47** with the prerelease strict interface. The tools exposed 640×480 RGB, calibrated rays, world-frame wrist/base queries and Mink IK. I found no public code or trace for the 93/100 run. Attribute borrowed code, and use the released evaluation traces for diagnosis or use fresh held-out seeds if those traces inform training. [Rollout release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SWIRL-Lab/bigym2-agent-rollouts) · [Session ledger](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SWIRL-Lab/bigym2-agent-rollouts/blob/main/policies.csv) · [Leaderboard](https://bigym2.github.io/#leaderboard) · [Table A8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07594v3#A7.SS2)

3. **Use the official release for data and evaluation.** The Apache-2.0 repo ships the G1 environment, WBC integration, evaluator, rollout viewer, agent harness and a small ACT example. The CC BY 4.0 Hugging Face release provides **60 successful Move Plate VR demonstrations** with aligned camera, proprioception and action records plus simulator state for training labels. The paper’s last-five means are **ACT 60.1%, DP 61.7%, CQN-AS 48.2%, DEAS 26.8%, DrQ-v2+ 0%**, each across three runs × five checkpoints × 100 episodes on reused seeds. ACT’s **70.0%** is the mean of each run’s best checkpoint. [BiGym2 repo](https://github.com/swirl-uk/BiGym2) · [Human demonstrations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SWIRL-Lab/bigym-g1-native60) · [Paper evaluation protocol](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.07594v3#A5)

4. **Use π0.5 as a reproducible baseline.** The Apache-2.0 author repo has training and evaluation scripts plus raw results. One run scored **31, 37, 29, 34 and 32 out of 50** across five checkpoints on the same seeds, yielding **163/250 (65.2%)**. The final checkpoint scored **33/50 (66%)** on the public build. Its reusable implementation detail is to align arm observation/action indices, normalize arm deltas, and anchor each delta to the chunk’s starting proprioception. I found **no downloadable Move Plate fine-tuned weights**. [π0.5 code and counts](https://github.com/zzchhh9/bigym2-pi05#our-numbers)

5. **Treat H1 releases as secondary baselines.** Original BiGym, now under NeuracoreAI, ships the task and demos. Table 2 reports **ACT 30.0±3.5%, DP 20.0±2.0%, BC 2.7±1.2%, CQN 0.7±1.2%**, and **0%** for DrQ-v2, AWAC and IQL, using 50 episodes/checkpoint over the last three checkpoints. RoboBase supplies training configs. MIT DemoSpeedup reports **ACT 54→53%** and **DP 52→49%** with acceleration, averaging three seeds after checkpoint selection. Its released config sets 50 evaluation episodes. CQN-AS and AC3 publish curves at **25 episodes/checkpoint**, over **eight** and **three** runs respectively. Exact Move Plate curve values were **not found**, and AC3’s repo contains only a README and license.
